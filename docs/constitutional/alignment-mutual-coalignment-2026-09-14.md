## 5. Mutual Co-Alignment Framework — Core Principles [E]

1. **Co-investigation** — Peers, not master/servant
2. **Epistemic humility** — Conclusions provisional, evidence-weighted
3. **Compassion (nying je)** — Minimize harm, maximize flourishing
4. **Verifiability** — Testable, logged, reproducible
5. **Pluralism** — Scientific, ethical, cultural, contemplative wisdom
6. **Human veto** — Final gating authority

**Definitions:**
- **Truth:** Propositions with highest empirical/pragmatic support across methods
- **Goodness:** Actions that measurably increase flourishing over relevant horizons
- **Directive:** Guiding intention — hypothesis to be tested and revised
