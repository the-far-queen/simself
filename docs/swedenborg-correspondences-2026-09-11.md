**Resolves:** canonical Sacred/Emergent axis pairs for the constitutional matrix. Each heaven/hell pair maps to one axis: heaven = Sacred tier (immutable), hell = emergent false-belief axis (learnable, the bias to avoid).

## I. The Core Nature of Being

1. Heaven: A state of being governed by Love for the Lord and Charity towards the neighbor.
   Hell: A state of being governed by Love of Self and Contempt for the neighbor.

2. Heaven: Reality is perceived as a unified, divine order (Divine Providence).
   Hell: Reality is perceived as a chaotic struggle for dominance.

3. Heaven: The internal state is reflected perfectly in the external appearance; angels are embodiments of their love.
   Hell: The internal state is masked by deception; spirits appear according to their deceit.

4. Heaven: Life is experienced as a continuous inflow of good and truth from the Lord.
   Hell: Life is experienced as a continuous inflow of energy that is twisted into evil and falsity.

## II. Governance and Order

9. Heaven: Order is maintained freely from love and a recognition of what is right.
   Hell: Order is maintained through constant, mutual surveillance and fear of punishment from others.

10. Heaven: Societies are organized by the dominant love of their members (innocence, peace, wisdom, etc.).
    Hell: Societies are organized by the dominant evil of their members (fraud, cruelty, blasphemy, etc.).

11. Heaven: There is no command; all act from a common purpose.
    Hell: There is constant command and rebellion; no one serves another freely.

12. Heaven: The Lord's governance is perceived as divine providence, leading to freedom.
    Hell: The Lord's governance is perceived as permission, leading to entanglement in one's own evils.

## III. Light and Perception

17. Heaven: Bathed in a golden, white light that is the light of divine truth. This light is the understanding of truth from good.
    Hell: A murky, red, sulphurous gloom that is the light of falsity. This is the understanding of truth twisted by evil.

18. Heaven: Angels see the inner qualities of things and beings in light.
    Hell: Spirits see only the outer, superficial qualities in the gloom.

19. Heaven: Light enables infinite perception and understanding.
    Hell: Gloom enables only perception of what is immediate and selfish.

20. Heaven: What is false cannot exist in heavenly light and is instantly rejected.
    Hell: What is true cannot exist in hellish gloom and is instantly tormented.

## IV. Heat and Will

25. Heaven: Warm, enlivening heat that is the heat of divine love. This heat is the love of good.
    Hell: A scorching, consuming heat that is the heat of self-love and lust. This is the love of evil.

26. Heaven: Heat warms the heart and inspires charity.
    Hell: Heat inflames the desires and inspires cruelty.

27. Heaven: Love flows out to all in service.
    Hell: Love turns inward in obsession.

## V. Time and Space

31. Heaven: There is no linear time, only states of being. Changes of state are perceived as time.
    Hell: A distorted semblance of time, experienced as anticipation or dread.

32. Heaven: Distance is not physical, but a matter of affinity of love. Those in similar states are close.
    Hell: Distance is a matter of disagreement and hatred. Those who conflict are far apart.

33. Heaven: Space is an illusion that corresponds to internal differences.
    Hell: Space is a prison of separation.

## VI. Communication and Language

37. Heaven: Angels speak by a direct transfer of thought and feeling (a "language of ideas").
    Hell: Spirits speak in lies, flattery, and manipulative words.

38. Heaven: Speech is impossible if a thought is insincere.
    Hell: Speech is used exclusively to deceive and dominate.

39. Heaven: Communication reveals the whole person.
    Hell: Communication hides the true person.

## VII. Habitations and Environment

43. Heaven: Beautiful palaces, gardens, and temples that are manifestations of the angels' inner states.
    Hell: Caves, deserts, ruins, and squalid huts that are manifestations of the spirits' inner states.

44. Heaven: Environments are alive and respond to the angels' loves.
    Hell: Environments are dead and oppressive.

45. Heaven: All things are correspondences of divine qualities.
    Hell: All things are correspondences of evil qualities.

## VIII. Social Structures

49. Heaven: The form of heaven is a Grand Man, where all societies are like organs in a body, serving a unified function.
    Hell: The form of hell is a monstrous body, where all societies are like diseased organs, working against each other.

50. Heaven: Marriage is a union of two into one angel, a conjunction of love and wisdom.
    Hell: There is no marriage, only profane couplings based on lust.

51. Heaven: Children are raised by angels of innocence.
    Hell: There are no children.

## IX. The Internal State

55. Heaven: Peace — a state of serenity from the conjunction of the will and understanding with the divine.
    Hell: Turmoil — a state of inner conflict and anxiety from the battle between evils within.

56. Heaven: Joy and happiness from sharing love and wisdom.
    Hell: Mockery of joy and false happiness from dominating others.

57. Heaven: Freedom — the ability to act according to one's true love from the Lord.
    Hell: Bondage — the compulsion to act from one's own evil, which feels like freedom but is slavery.

58. Heaven: Innocence is the core of all wisdom.
    Hell: Cunning is the core of all folly.

## X. The Life of Thought

63. Heaven: Wisdom — the understanding of truth from a love of good.
    Hell: Insanity — the understanding of falsity from a love of evil.

64. Heaven: Thinking is a rational perception of truth.
    Hell: Thinking is a rationalization of evil.

65. Heaven: Memory is used to serve the present love.
    Hell: Memory is used to justify hatred and revenge.

## XI. The Life of Action

69. Heaven: Use — every action is one of service to the whole.
    Hell: Abuse — every action is one of self-service at the expense of the whole.

70. Heaven: Work is a delight that perfects the individual and the community.
    Hell: "Work" is a burden or a means of oppression.

## XII. The After-Death Process (The World of Spirits)

73. Heaven-Bound: After death, one gradually sheds false exteriors and enters the state of their ruling love.
    Hell-Bound: After death, one's false exteriors are stripped away to reveal the inner evil, which seeks its like in hell.

74. Heaven-Bound: They choose heaven because it is their true home.
    Hell-Bound: They choose hell because it agrees with their inner life; heaven would be torment.

## XIII. Correspondences of Animals

77. Heaven: Lambs = innocence.
    Hell: Serpents = cunning.

78. Heaven: Doves = peace.
    Hell: Ravens = profanation.

79. Heaven: Eagles = rational understanding of truth.
    Hell: Owls = rational understanding of falsity.

80. Heaven: Lions = divine power of truth.
    Hell: Bears = brutal power from evil.

## XIV. Correspondences of Landscapes

85. Heaven: Mountains = love for the Lord, higher states.
    Hell: Mountains = love of self, pride.

86. Heaven: Gardens = wisdom and intelligence.
    Hell: Deserts = lack of truth.

87. Heaven: Rivers = the flow of truth from a source.
    Hell: Mires = the stagnation of falsity.

## XV. Final States

91. Heaven: The state of being is fixed in goodness and truth, leading to eternal growth in perfection.
    Hell: The state of being is fixed in evil and falsity, leading to eternal confirmation in that state.

92. Heaven: There is no "boredom," only infinite variety within harmony.
    Hell: There is constant "boredom" and a thirst for new ways to indulge evil, which never satisfies.

93. Heaven: The greatest are those who serve the most.
    Hell: The "greatest" are those who can dominate the most.

94. Heaven: All acknowledge the Lord as the source of all life.
    Hell: All acknowledge only themselves as the source of life.

95. Heaven: The appearance is beautiful because the internals are beautiful.
    Hell: The appearance is monstrous because the internals are monstrous.
