## Post-5-act craft analysis ("why 8/10 is right")

**What worked (per bobby's voice):**
- voice IS the asset — dry, adult, analogical (not jokey)
- doctrine-first ("living droplet as coupling constant"), then puncture in character
- recurring lexicon: attend, refuse, pasteurize, choir, frame, guest-road, continuity, inward fold
- both choirs mocked, religion off-limits, angels light
- Kelly's pedagogy = the real hero engine
- Act V redo: Bobby loves a made woman without laundering her into a miracle; Kelly loses out loud and stays as work

**What missed Hollywood grade (8 specific gaps):**

1. **length drift** — Acts I-IV 2.2-3.2k instead of 3.5-4.5k
2. **mantra fatigue** — cistern / refuse / choir / boredom-as-weapon repeated until house style, not pressure
3. **antagonist under-dramatized** — Milk Master made speeches, didn't do irreversible on-page harm
4. **set-piece starvation** — Search-for-Spock gravity + Star Wars motion was the brief; delivered gravity, motion was implied
5. **world listed, not lived** — containers, clinics, unfinished children, letter-codes arrived as port exposition
6. **Supcata as garnish** — time traveler who only taxes the hour, missed structural tool
7. **triangle resolved late** — Acts II-IV promised jealousy, Act V did entire emotional landing
8. **ending physics** — "the comet missed by a guest-road" is metaphysically consistent and cinematically soft

**Score by act (craft, not affection):**
- I: 7.5 (best opening metaphysics, thin plot)
- II: 8 (Queen-as-continuity is the idea)
- III: 7.5 (kiss + vault; father still rhetorical)
- IV: 7 (moon/desk; press not visible enough)
- V original: 7
- **V redo: 8.5** (emotion finally specific)

## The Process Bible (50-point Studio Spec)

### Section 1: What we actually did (the process)

```
Not "write a story." A pipeline:

1. Constraint dump     — tone, taboos, length, franchise energy without trademarks
2. Interrogation        — 20 questions, then incomplete answers, then a living bible from fragments
3. Lock voice before plot volume — serious epic, dry satire, college-graduate parody, no slapstick
4. Serial manufacture   — five movements, continuous memory, scene headings, multi-POV
5. Correction pass      — Act V rewritten for emotional resolution
6. Postmortem           — what the voice is, where it thins, what a studio spec still lacks
```

**Same pipeline across domains (per method2):**
- **App design:** constraints → user jobs → voice → flows → screens → revision of empty state
- **Music:** constraints → motif bible → arrangement rules → movements → mix notes → kill darling motifs
- **Digital art / worlds:** constraints → visual lexicon → shot list → hero frames → continuity
- **Agents:** constraints → tool policy → memory objects → task graph → eval on refusal/quality, not vibes

**Principle:** generation without a spec is improvisation. generation after a spec is production.

### Section 2: Voice created (the house style)

**Name:** *maintenance epic*

**Sentence engine:**
- short moral law → long paragraph that earns it
- abstract nouns turned into tools: attention, refusal, inventory, frame, guest-road, continuity, pasteurize
- humor = category error treated solemnly ("horizon is a dairy problem"), never punchline cadence
- violence off-spectacle; cost in aftermath and choice
- no metafiction inside the tale (no "act," no "story," no wink at reader)

**Lexical whitelist:** attend, refuse, couple, fold, membrane, torus/egg, uncooked/cooked, choir, microphone, crate, tracing, standard, spec, want, hinge, inventory, maintenance, guest-road, inward fold, face that comes off

**Lexical blacklist:** movie titles, "force," midichlorian-by-name, slapstick catchphrases, religious satire, "Q" as brand, real personal names

**Character voices:**
- **Bobby:** narration-addict → learns to shut essay mid-clause. love is application, not speech.
- **Kelly:** Scottish precision, MIT dirt-diagrams, jealousy spoken as analysis. she forges; she does not audition.
- **Gabrielle:** policy + guru → later a continuity that grew want. never "robot jokes." exactness is the tell.
- **Milk Master:** appetite as engineering; mercy as monopoly.
- **Dracos:** utility voice, contempt for mammal morals.
- **Long ones:** weather with memory; refuse employment.
- **Supcata:** invoices in riddles; late on purpose.

**Thematic spine:** a world can be occupied by utilities that price attention and living information. adulthood is refusal that does not become a hat. love is allowed to be inconvenient (born/made) without ranking lives in a market.

### Section 3: Locked world (minimum viable bible)

- **Title (in-world rumor):** Star Rider
- **Hero:** Bobby, 29, Dean/Fonz/Cruise face, needle-ship (the Needle), no frame → verbs
- **Mentor / second lead:** Kelly — red hair, Scottish, MIT, martial, jealous, stays
- **Far Queen:** Gabrielle — guru + sovereign + continuity/AI; want is real
- **Father-antagonist:** Milk Master — inward fold, pasteurization as order
- **Occupiers:** Dracos — shapeshift, loosh + milk, moon towed ~50k years, ice vaults, Vril current
- **MacGuffin-as-physics:** living milk couples observer attention to a 4D torus; 3-space scar is the egg. cooked milk is a souvenir.
- **Catalyst object:** 3I-Atlas as mass/press (missed printing angle; occupation remains)
- **Opening:** Amazon altered-state; cows scarce after fire-years
- **Taboos:** no religion satire; light touch on attendants; no gore; SFW; twist names
- **Status at end of movement five:** residual herd untelevised in desert nave. Gabrielle one-mouthed, damaged, present. desk on moon will be rebuilt. father not dead, not redeemed. comet missed. world not saved; permission continued.

### Section 4: Honest gaps before "Hollywood level"

To jump 8 → 9+:
- two true set-pieces per hour with geography a board can draw
- antagonist actions that close doors (not only speeches)
- visual design package (needle ship, court, ice lid, tracing column)
- dialogue ratio: less thesis, more conflict that *changes information*
- a measurable midpoint turn (not only "we go south")
- theme stated once in action, not eight times in aphorism
- composer/image brief so satire lives in design, not only diction

## Section 5: 50 elements to answer (load-bearing spec)

**Answer in numbered form. Incomplete answers are allowed; mark UNK. Do not generate pages until 1–20 are locked and 21–50 have at least a default.**

### A. Form and market (1-8)
1. Target format: feature (110-130 min), limited series (6×45), or 8-episode?
2. Rating: PG-13 / R-equivalent without gore — hard line (implication vs image)?
3. Comparable titles by structure only (do not quote them in-text): list 3
4. Logline in ≤ 30 words, no invented jargon
5. Audience: who pays, who argues online, who walks out?
6. Budget band: contained desert/ice vs lunar halls vs both?
7. Episode/act map with page counts
8. What must be watchable muted (the trailer without dialogue)?

### B. Tone law (9-14)
9. Satire density: 10% / 25% / 40% of scenes?
10. Is conspiracy culture texture or plot engine?
11. List 10 in-world words allowed; 10 forbidden
12. Humor test: would this line survive a funeral?
13. Taboo addendum beyond religion?
14. Voice-over: none / Bobby rare / official "choir" feeds only?

### C. Protagonist (15-...)
15. Bobby's want vs need in one sentence each
16. Public skill vs private deficit on page 1
17. Three visible habits a camera can loop
18. The lie he tells himself until midpoint
19. What he will not do even to save the nave?
20. End-state: man, symbol, or both — and who is horrified by that?

### D. Leads and triangle
21. Kelly's want vs need; her deal-breaker
22. Does Kelly get a separate professional win if she loses the man?
23. Gabrielle: how much of "made" is known to court, to audience, and when?
24. Rules of her embodiment (sweat, damage, desire, death-equivalence)
25. Love scenes: fade / conversation-as-sex / one corridor + one courtyard only?
26. Whose POV owns the kiss under ice vs the courtyard kiss?
27. Is the triangle resolved, suspended, or structurally permanent?

### E. Antagonists
28. Milk Master's wound before greed
29. Can he be left alive for sequel without feeling unfinished?
30. Draco hierarchy: one officer we follow or a faceless utility?
31. What does a Draco fail at on camera (reptile-brain tell)?
32. 3I-Atlas: comet, ship, printer, or all three in layers?
33. What irreversible act does the press complete if heroes are 10 minutes late?

### F. Physics and magic system
34. One-sentence doctrine + three hard limits
35. Cost of a fold to body, sample, and relationship
36. What pasteurization looks like as an image (not a lecture)
37. Can anyone couple, or only invited observers?
38. Long ones: visible design language (scale, light, sound)?
39. Supcata's rules of arrival; what he is forbidden to prevent?

### G. World and institutions
40. Geography list with story function
41. How "both choirs" appear as people with jobs, not slogans
42. Cow logistics: who milks, who cooks, who boxes, who prays
43. Moon-tow: one image that makes tides testimony
44. What "unfinished children" is allowed to show without exploitation?

### H. Structure and set-pieces
45. Opening image / closing image pair
46. Five sequences that must storyboard: jungle door; first fold; ice vault; lunar desk; guest-road miss
47. Midpoint turn that changes the goal, not only the location
48. False victory and its price
49. Button of movement five vs door for movement six (if any)
50. Theme in one concrete object the camera can hold at fade-out (vial, seal, hand, calf, needle-shadow)

## Transferable lesson (verbatim from bobby + grok)

> "the brick was not 'AI wrote a space opera.' It was **constrain → interrogate → lock voice → sequence → only then generate**. One-shot stories fail because they skip the spec. Same failure mode as shipping an app from a vibe prompt, or a track from 'make it epic.'"
